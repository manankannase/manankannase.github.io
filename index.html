Multilingual record linkage

SAL-Det

Semantic-aware latent DiffusionDet

I reproduced DiffusionDet (CVPR 2023) end to end, then added CLIP-guided box initialisation, diffusion in latent space and a score-aware box renewal policy. It reaches 44.67 AP on COCO in 32K iterations, close to the paper’s 45.8 at 450K.

NilVanta

Order matching and recovery lab

A fixed-capacity limit order book on an indexed red-black tree, with GTC, IOC and FOK matching, a simulated OMS, ITCH replay and a journal that is written and synced before every command. A separate Python oracle cross-checks every match.
